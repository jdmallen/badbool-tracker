Privacy

← Back to the tracker

By default, nothing leaves your browser

Your progress — which sites you've requested removal from, your notes, timestamps — is stored only in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server unless you sign in. Clearing your browser's site data erases it, so export a backup (the "Export JSON" button) before you do that.

If you sign in with GitHub

To sync your progress across devices, we store:

  • A per-app opaque user id assigned by the sign-in provider (different for this app than for any other app you've signed into with GitHub) — never your GitHub username or email.
  • Your site statuses, notes, and timestamps (the same data kept locally).

This is stored in Azure Table Storage. It is not end-to-end encrypted — the site operator can technically read it. Signing in sets one authentication cookie; there is no analytics, no ads, and no third-party scripts on this site.

Advice: don't put passwords, full Social Security numbers, or other ID numbers in your notes.

Deleting your data

Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy. Then use "Delete my cloud data" in the tracker header while signed in — this permanently removes your row from cloud storage. Your local browser copy is unaffected unless you also use "Clear all progress."
